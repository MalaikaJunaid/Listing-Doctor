How we calculate these scores

Completeness checks for eight detail groups: price, location, bedrooms, bathrooms, floor area, tenure, fees, and availability/viewing. It is the share of those groups detected in your text.

Clarity uses average words per sentence. Averages from 8–20 words score 100; subtract 4 points per word below 8 or 3 points per word above 20, down to a minimum of 0.

Searchability checks for seven useful listing keyword groups: property type, location, beds, baths, floor area, features, and nearby amenities. It is the share detected.

Missing critical fields counts absent price, location, bedroom count, bathroom count, and floor area. These are text heuristics—not portal rankings or a substitute for checking listing requirements.
